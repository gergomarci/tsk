There was once a brief and beautiful moment when opening a web page resulted in the web page appearing.

Today, arriving somewhere online can feel more like entering a negotiation. Would you like the newsletter? Can we send notifications? Are you sure you don't want the newsletter? Perhaps you'd prefer twenty percent off something you weren't planning to buy?

None of these questions are especially difficult. Finding the tiny button that means “please let me continue what I was doing,” however, has become something of a competitive design discipline.
